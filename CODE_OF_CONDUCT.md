# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be civil and stay on the technical substance. Assume the other person is acting
in good faith and arguing about the work rather than about you.

Not welcome here: personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory or sexualised
language, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of
a discussion.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
other space belonging to this project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what falls short of this standard and may edit, hide, or
delete a contribution, lock a thread, or block an account. Report a concern
privately to the maintainer through GitHub rather than in the thread.

Enforcement is at the maintainer's discretion. This is a personal project, not
an organisation with an appeals process.
